The ensemble grew out of an idea by **Valentina Oriani**, its founder: to bring together singers united by a passion for sacred and choral repertoire. Our exploration spans centuries of tradition — from the Renaissance polyphony of Palestrina and Victoria to Baroque motets, through to contemporary sacred works by composers such as Arvo Pärt and James MacMillan.
