The ensemble began with **Valentina Oriani**'s wish to bring a choir to life. She called on a few musician friends and, within the Bocconi University community, sought out people from all over the world who wanted to sing. The result is a deeply intercultural ensemble — voices from Ireland, the United States, Estonia, Turkey and Italy — held together by the wish to sing together, something close to a missionary impulse towards beauty.

Our repertoire spans five centuries — from the villancicos of the Spanish Renaissance, such as Juan del Encina's *¿A quién debo yo llamar?* and the anonymous *Verbum caro factum est*, to the chorale *O Jesulein süß* in Bach's harmonisation, and on to traditional songs from Ireland (*Suantraí*), Catalonia (*Sant Josep i la Mare de Déu*) and Bohemia (*Rocking*), and Alfred Burt's American carols.
